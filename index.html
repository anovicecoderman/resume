Projects

Follow the Money

Case competition, PayU, 2026

  • I designed a way for PayU to catch merchants who hide illegal betting behind genuine paperwork, while protecting honest ones.
  • I wrote the first solution by hand. Then one AI attacked it as a red team and another AI fixed the weak points as a blue team, over five rounds.
  • I ran a data test in a Python notebook on 1,000,000 synthetic payments. Reviewing 0.63% of them caught about half the fraud cases. The data is synthetic and the fraud is cash-out fraud, not betting, so real results will differ.
  • I put it in an 8-slide deck and a 2-minute video.

Apple DCF model

Excel, 2015–2024 data

  • I built a discounted cash flow model of Apple from its 2015–2024 financials, with a forecast for 2025–2033 and a terminal value.
  • I made two versions: one with a flat growth rate and one with a tapering growth rate.
  • The first result showed Apple as overvalued. Changing the rates changed the answer, so I learned how much a DCF depends on its assumptions.
  • It is a basic model with no operating drivers. I built it to understand the data and the concept.
